Title: Common period myths
Keywords: myth, myths, pamahiin, bawal, maligo, bathe, bath, shower, ligo, wash hair, maasim, sour, exercise, swim, totoo ba
---
Bathing and washing your hair during your period are safe and help you stay clean and comfortable. Warm
showers may even ease cramps. Eating sour food does not stop or worsen a period, and light exercise or
swimming is fine and can help with cramps. If a belief makes you uncomfortable or worried, it's okay to ask
a healthcare professional what is safe for you.
