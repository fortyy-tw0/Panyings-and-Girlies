Title: Your first period
Keywords: first period, first time, menarche, start, began, teen, young, kid, unang regla, first mens, nagka-regla
---
Most people get their first period between about ages 10 and 15, often around two years after breasts start
to develop. The first periods may be light, irregular, and spaced far apart, which is normal for the first
few years. It helps to keep a pad in your bag and to talk with a trusted adult. See a healthcare professional
if periods haven't started by age 15, or if bleeding is very heavy or painful.
