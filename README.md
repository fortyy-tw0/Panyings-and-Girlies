# SikLo

**A private, offline-first menstrual cycle tracker with an on-device AI companion.**

SikLo (from the Filipino *siklo*, "cycle") helps you track your period, symptoms, and reproductive health without sending your sensitive data to the cloud. Your records stay on your phone, and the AI companion runs locally. It only reads your data after you give permission.

## Features

- **Cycle tracking:** log period dates, flow, cramps, symptoms, mood, sleep, energy, and notes.
- **Deterministic predictions:** the app's own rule-based logic calculates your next period, average cycle length, cycle variation, fertile window, and ovulation estimate from your history. The AI never makes up dates.
- **Local AI companion:** ask questions like *"Summarize my symptoms this cycle"* or *"What changed compared with my last cycles?"* and get plain-language answers. It uses careful wellness language and points you to a health professional when that's the right call.
- **Contraception mode (optional):** track your method, set reminders, keep a usage history, and read general educational information.
- **Pregnancy mode:** switch to a gestational-week timeline with a symptom journal, appointment reminders, notes, and a list of questions for your next doctor visit.

## Privacy first

- Data is stored on-device and encrypted. No account is required.
- AI processing happens on-device, so nothing is sent to a cloud AI provider.
- Consent is granular: you choose which data the AI can read, and you can revoke access at any time.
- You can export or permanently delete your data whenever you want.
- Your data is never sold or shared, and it is never used to train models.

## Disclaimer

SikLo is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Predictions are estimates and are less reliable for irregular cycles. SikLo does not guarantee contraceptive effectiveness, and its pregnancy information is educational only. It does not replace professional medical or prenatal care.
