Title: Food, iron, and your period
Keywords: food, eat, diet, iron, anemia, tired, fatigue, dizzy, pagkain, kain, vegetables, malunggay, gulay, energy
---
Losing blood each month can lower iron, especially with heavy periods, which can leave you tired or dizzy.
Iron-rich foods include meat, fish, eggs, beans, and leafy greens like malunggay and kangkong, and eating
them with vitamin C foods such as fruit helps your body absorb iron. Drinking enough water and eating regular
meals can also help with energy. See a healthcare professional if you often feel very tired, weak, or dizzy.
