Title: Spotting between periods
Keywords: spotting, spot, light bleeding, between periods, brown discharge, mid cycle, patak, patak-patak
---
Spotting is light bleeding outside your usual period, often pink or brown. It can happen around ovulation,
when starting or changing hormonal birth control, or during times of stress. It is usually lighter than a
period and may not need a pad. See a healthcare professional if spotting happens often, comes with pain,
happens after sex, or happens after menopause.
